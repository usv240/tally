## 2. Judge walkthrough (Judge mode, no login)

About four minutes.

1. Open the live URL. Read the hero. Press "Try the live demo."
2. Judge mode loads Rosa's home at 07:38 Tuesday. Press "Play the day."
3. Roll call plays as audio with a transcript. Watch attendance fill in and the ratio indicator show "12 at 3:10, within limits."
4. Breakfast photo. See items, confidences, and the green verdict.
5. Snack photo. See the question "Is that milk or juice?" Choose "Milk." See the verdict.
6. Lunch photo. See "Lunch needs a fruit. You have oranges." Press "Add orange slices and re-take." See the fixed plate and the linked record.
7. Afternoon snack. Hear the allergy alert. See the substitution logged for Nia.
8. Advance to 18:30. See the digest: six notes, one question, one nudge. Approve notes, answer the question, tap "drill done."
9. Open Month. See the claim total, the line breakdown, the export, and "Send to sponsor."
10. Open Trace Viewer. Expand the snack event: vision output, rules verdict with rule version, the decision to ask.
11. Reset. Optionally, use the sandbox key to POST the breakfast photo yourself with the provided curl.

Everything is real system behavior on the deployed stack. The only fixtures are the five photos and the pre-recorded roll call audio, which the page says.
