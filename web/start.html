Set up your home

Three steps, about three minutes. Nothing is saved until you finish, and no note goes to any family until you approve it.

1. Your children 2. Your licence 3. Your sponsor

Paste whatever list you already have

An enrolment form, the note on the fridge, the spreadsheet your sponsor sent. Names, birthdays, days, subsidy and allergies are read out of it. Anything that cannot be read is shown to you rather than dropped, because a child missing from the roster is a child whose allergy is never checked.

Birthdays in any shape: 3 March 2025, March 3 2025, 2025-03-03 or 03/03/25. Only the word "allergy" or "allergic" makes something an allergy, so "Maya loves milk" is not one.

Your licence

This decides the ratio and group size Tally holds you to, and which meal patterns apply.

Only Texas has a ratio table so far. Tally refuses to guess a limit it does not have, because a wrong limit is worse than no answer.

Who do you claim through?

Your sponsor receives the monthly claim. Nothing is ever sent until you review the month and press send.

That is everything.

Tomorrow morning, say who is here and photograph the first plate. Tally will speak after each one, and will ask you at most the number of questions you set.

This is the setup flow, and it stops here on purpose. Saving a new home would write to the shared demo, so nothing is written. Open the demo to see a home that is already running.
